A make.fun entry for the [Texts From Your Future Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-texts-from-your-future-self-dd44595e) competition.

`main.ts` writes one funny text message from the writer's future self to `/output/entry.txt` — under 200 characters, no setup, just the message as it would arrive on your phone.

## The message

> Future you: stop looking for your keys. They're in the inside pocket of the jacket you're wearing. You've checked it twice and missed them both times.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```
